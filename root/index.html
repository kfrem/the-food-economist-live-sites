About

The Food Economist is the practice of Godfred Frimpong, a trade and food economist with an MSc in Food Economics and Marketing and more than thirty years in finance, cost analysis and business development across the UK and Africa.

The work is deliberately narrow: written cost, margin and statutory-fee analysis for businesses that sell food. Not software you have to configure, not a retainer you cannot leave, and not a report that tells you what you already knew. Every figure in a written report is traceable to the assumption it came from.

No compliance scheme, supplier, broker or software vendor pays a commission on anything recommended here. That independence is the point of the service.
