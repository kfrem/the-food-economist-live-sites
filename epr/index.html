Questions

Asked before commissioning

Is this a compliance scheme?

No. Compliance schemes register you and submit your data. This desk does the piece they don't: forecasting what the liability does to your cash flow and budget, independently of anyone selling you membership.

My data is messy. Is that a problem?

No. Rough tonnages, supplier invoices, screenshots and spreadsheets are all workable. Part of the engagement is organising what you have into figures you can defend.

Do you provide tax or legal services?

No. This is independent economic forecasting and cost analysis. The output is designed to hand to your finance adviser and board, and works alongside whatever compliance scheme you use.

What if I'm under the £2m / 50-tonne threshold?

You may only have reporting duties for now — but the Snapshot will tell you how close you are to the threshold and what crossing it would cost, so the liability never arrives as a surprise.

Prefer to start with a question?

Everything is handled in writing. Send a message and you will receive a written reply, usually within one working day. A short call is available on request.
