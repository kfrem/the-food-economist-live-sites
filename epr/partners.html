How the referral works

  1. You introduce the client by email, with permission, or pass them this page and the sample.
  2. The client completes written intake. There is no sales-call requirement.
  3. We confirm scope in writing and the client pays by Stripe or invoice before work begins.
  4. For an accepted referral arrangement, a referral reward is agreed in writing before any introduction is made.
Important boundaries. The desk is independent economic modelling and written analysis. It is not a compliance scheme, legal advice, tax advice or a statutory calculation. The client retains responsibility for regulatory reporting and decisions.
