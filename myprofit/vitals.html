£1,200 a month

Renegotiating payment terms with core suppliers from 15 to 65 days moved this operator from cash-negative to cash-positive. The working capital that freed up was then used to buy on cash-discount terms elsewhere.

Independent restaurant
£25–£60 a day

Off delivery costs, across both the delivery platforms and supplier transport: about £25 on a quiet day, £60 on a busy one. Roughly £650 to £1,560 a month on a six-day week.

Café
£1,500–£3,000 a month

One kitchen was binning the equivalent of 10 to 20 covers every other day, at about £10 a cover. Added up, that is £1,500 to £3,000 a month in the bin. Seeing it was the first step to cutting it.
