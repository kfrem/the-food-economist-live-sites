What the report is designed to reveal

Until live client case studies exist, the honest proof is the analysis method and the visible sample. These are the pressure points the written report tests.

  • Supplier lines rising faster than menu prices.
  • Labour-heavy shifts that look busy but erase margin.
  • Delivery/platform sales that grow revenue while weakening net result.
  • One or two menu items carrying a hidden monthly leak.

Questions

Asked before commissioning

My books are a mess. Can you still help?

Yes — that's the normal case, not the exception. Bank app screenshots, a folder of supplier invoices and your till summary are enough for the Triage. Part of the job is turning a mess into figures you can act on.

Is this a filing or records service?

No. This is independent economic analysis of your costs and margins. It works alongside your existing advisers — the report is written so you can hand it straight to them, or to your bank.

How is £95 + VAT enough to be serious?

Because it's a written triage, not a turnaround project. It finds the leak and prices the fixes. If your situation needs the deeper menu-level work, the £95 + VAT is credited in full against the £395 + VAT Diagnostic — so the triage costs nothing if you proceed.

Will you try to sell me software or suppliers?

No. The desk is independent by design: no subscriptions, no affiliate commissions, no supplier deals. If software would genuinely help you, the report says so and names neutral options — that's all.

Prefer to start with a question?

Everything is handled in writing. Send a message and you will get a written reply, usually within one working day.
